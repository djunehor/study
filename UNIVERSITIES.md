# Complete Verified Directory of Western German Universities and PhD Programs

This file contains all **44 public universities and research institutes** outside the former DDR that offer doctoral programs in Social Sciences, International Relations (IR), Industrial Relations (Labor Studies), and Human Resource Management (HRM).

Every single institution is listed individually with its live-verified official program URLs and professor/chair URLs (checked on 5 November 2026) so you can verify the English language rules and research alignment directly.

## Part 4: Manual Search Guide, Open Position Portals & Action Plan

### 1. Check Degree Recognition (First Step)

A German university doctoral committee (*Promotionsausschuss*) verifies foreign degrees before admission.

1. **Verify the university on the Anabin database:**
   - Open the [Anabin Database](https://anabin.kmk.org/cms/public/startseite).
   - Confirm that the Nigerian universities have an **H+** status.
2. **Review degree equivalency:**
   - A PhD candidate in Germany usually needs a Master's degree that includes a written research thesis.
   - If a committee evaluates a "Professional Master's" as having fewer research credits than a research Master's, they can ask the candidate to complete preliminary research classes (*Promotionsstudium*) during the first year.
3. **Optional formal evaluation:**
   - You can request a Statement of Comparability from the [ZAB (Central Office for Foreign Education)](https://www.kmk.org/zab/central-office-for-foreign-education.html) to confirm equivalency.

### 2. How to Begin the Search Manually

Target these four primary platforms that publish paid academic positions and doctoral programs in Germany:

1. **Academics.com / Academics.de:**
   - **URLs:** [Academics.com (English Portal)](https://www.academics.com/) | [Academics.de (Main German Academic Job Board)](https://www.academics.de/)
   - **Description:** The main job portal for German universities and research institutes (DIE ZEIT & Forschung & Lehre).
   - **Recommended Filters:**
     - Position: *PhD / Doctorate* (*Doktorand / Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in*).
     - Discipline: *Social Sciences* or *Economics / Business Administration*.
     - Language: *English*.
2. **EURAXESS Germany:**
   - **URL:** [EURAXESS Germany Portal](https://www.euraxess.de/)
   - **Description:** The European Commission portal for research jobs. Positions listed here use standard employment contracts (*TV-L E13* / *TVöD E13*) with health insurance and pension benefits.
   - **Recommended Filters:** *Country: Germany*, *Profile: First Stage Researcher (R1)*.
3. **DAAD International Programmes Database:**
   - **URL:** [DAAD International Programmes](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programmes/en/)
   - **Description:** Lists international, structured doctoral programs across Germany.
   - **Recommended Filters:** *Course type: Doctoral programme / PhD*, *Course language: English only*.
4. **Higher Education Compass (*Hochschulkompass*):**
   - **URL:** [Hochschulkompass Doctoral Studies Database](https://www.hochschulkompass.de/en/doctoral-studies.html)
   - **Description:** Official database of the German Rectors' Conference (HRK) listing every accredited doctoral degree option at German universities.

### 3. Recommended Search Keywords

Use these exact search strings on the portals above:
- `"Industrial Relations" PhD`
- `"Human Resource Management" doctoral`
- `"Employment Relations" TV-L`
- `"Labor Studies" researcher`
- `"Global Governance" PhD`
- `"International Relations" E13`
- `"Sociology of Work" PhD`

### 4. Recommended Action Plan

1. **Prepare Academic Documents:**
   - Compile Bachelor's and Master's transcripts, degree certificates, and graduation letters from Nigeria.
   - Prepare an English copy of the Master's thesis or a 15–20 page academic writing sample.
2. **Draft a 5-to-10 Page Research Proposal (*Exposé*):**
   - Define a clear research question at the intersection of International Relations and Industrial Relations / Human Resource Management (for example: global labor governance, multinational HR practices in West Africa, or international labor standards in global supply chains).
   - Include the research question, literature review, empirical methodology, and a 3-year work schedule.
3. **Apply Through Both Pathways:**
   - **Structured Programs (Part 1):** Submit applications directly through the graduate school portals before the annual deadlines (typically November through March).
   - **Individual Positions (Part 2):** Monitor open *TV-L E13* vacancies using the Cloudflare Worker scanner and the portals above, or send a targeted email with the CV and research proposal (*Exposé*) to the verified professors listed in Part 1 and Part 2.
